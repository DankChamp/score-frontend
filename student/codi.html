Can you explain how to find HCF using Euclid's algorithm?

Just now

Euclid's division algorithm finds the HCF of two numbers by repeatedly applying the division lemma:

  1. Divide the larger number by the smaller
  2. If remainder is 0, the divisor is the HCF
  3. If remainder ≠ 0, divide the previous divisor by the remainder
  4. Repeat until remainder is 0

For 135 and 225:
225 = 135 × 1 + 90
135 = 90 × 1 + 45
90 = 45 × 2 + 0
HCF = 45
